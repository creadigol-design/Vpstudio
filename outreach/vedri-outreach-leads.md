## The thesis (from the brief)

We're targeting ambitious brands with exciting products but clear signs their organic social isn't working. When organic underperforms, brands lean on paid ads to compensate — and because thousands of brands are bidding for the same audience, ad space gets more expensive and every acquisition costs more. It still returns an ROI, so founders rarely question it.

The brands that get it right build **fandom** — they keep their audience warm, engaged and ready to buy *before* the ad is ever served, through consistent entertainment ("always-on"). The blocker to always-on is almost always **production**: good video, made often, is slow and expensive. That's vedrí's angle — a virtual production studio that makes high-volume, high-quality video affordable (multi-cam, many setups per shoot day, instant background/scene swaps, minimal post).

## How the list is ranked

Weighted to favour **Wales / North West / Midlands** (travel-warm to DocShed). The first six are in-region — two are North Wales bullseyes. The last four are national brands where the ambition-vs-social gap was too stark to leave out.
